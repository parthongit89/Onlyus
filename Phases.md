# Project Development Phases

## Global Development Rules
- Development must follow the phases in order.
- Do not skip any phase.
- Complete, test, and review each phase before moving to the next.
- Do not implement future-phase features early.
- Maintain backward compatibility unless explicitly instructed.

---

## UI/UX Implementation Rule
- All UI/UX designs will be provided by the Project Owner in tameplates folder.
- The AI may be for any falults or mis match contents as grammer redesign any screen.
- The AI must may be modify colors, spacing, typography, icons, layouts, animations, or user flows unless instructed.
- The AI must implement the provided designs exactly.
- If any design is missing or unclear, the AI must ask for clarification instead of making assumptions.

### Design Priority
1. Figma Design
2. Design Images
3. Wireframes
4. Written Instructions
